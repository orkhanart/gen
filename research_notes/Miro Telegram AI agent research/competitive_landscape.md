## 1. What is "Miro" (Mira), the product we are comparing against?

### Takeaway
No Telegram AI agent called "Miro" exists in search results. The product is **Mira** (Telegram handle **@mira**, website **mira.tg**, docs **wiki.mira.tg**, X **@trymira**), built by **The Open Platform (TOP, top.co)**. It is a Telegram-native, multi-model assistant whose main selling points are **group-chat participation with scoped memory**, **800+ app integrations via Composio**, a **Cocoon-powered Private Mode**, scheduled **Skills**, and a **TON agentic wallet** that is still on testnet.

### Inferences
- Mira's positioning is "the AI teammate in your Telegram groups, with memory", plus a creator and media toolkit and a crypto/TON angle. The design story a candidate can build on: **group-native, privacy-scoped memory** is Mira's moat claim; **trust, pricing clarity and the action-confirmation UX** are its visible weak points.
- The inconsistent integration counts, wallet status and pricing tiers across Mira's own surfaces suggest the product is moving fast and has **information-architecture and pricing-communication debt**. That is a natural opening for a senior product designer's proposal.

## 2. Direct competitors inside Telegram

### Takeaway
Mira's most serious "competitor" in Telegram is **Telegram itself**. Between January and May 2026 Telegram shipped native AI summaries, a native AI Editor and Cocoon private inference, and opened the platform to any AI bot through Guest Mode, bot-to-bot chats, Chat Automation (a bot replies on your behalf) and native streaming and threads. Around that sit **Grok** (a Premium-gated, contested xAI deal), **Perplexity's @askplexbot**, a long tail of **multi-model ChatGPT wrapper bots** (e.g. GPT4Telegrambot), **self-hosted agents** (OpenClaw, Hermes Agent) with Telegram as their main remote control, **Telegram Business AI responders**, and **TON-native agentic wallets**.

### Cited Findings

**Telegram native AI (first-party)**
- **Cocoon** (Confidential Compute Open Network) is a decentralized network for AI inference in TEEs on TON, announced by Durov at Blockchain Life 2025 in Dubai (late 2025). Telegram's message translation was routed through it — [Durov's Code](https://durovscode.com/telegram-launches-cocoon-decentralized-ai-network). The official repo says: "GPU owners earn TON by serving models; app developers plug into low-cost, secure and verifiable AI compute" — [TelegramMessenger/cocoon README](https://github.com/TelegramMessenger/cocoon)
- **AI Summaries (Jan 2026).** Summaries of channel posts and Instant View pages, run on open-source models on Cocoon with each request encrypted. Telegram did not disclose which models — [Telegram blog: AI Summaries, New Design](https://telegram.org/blog/new-design-ai-summaries); [CyberInsider](https://cyberinsider.com/telegram-introduces-privacy-focused-ai-summaries-backed-by-cocoon/); [TestingCatalog](https://www.testingcatalog.com/telegram-adds-ai-summaries-powered-by-cocoon-network/)
- **AI Editor (v12.6, March 31, 2026).** Built-in grammar fixes, style rewrites and translation in one step. Reported styles include Formal, Short, Zen and Viking, and some outlets add Tribal, Corp and Biblical. An "AI" icon appears after about 3 lines of text. Runs on Cocoon — [Telegram blog: AI Editor, Mighty Polls, Live Photos, Bots Managed by Bots](https://telegram.org/blog/ai-editor-mighty-polls-and-more); [NewsBytes](https://www.newsbytesapp.com/news/science/telegram-s-new-update-adds-ai-text-editor-improves-polls/story); [Pasquale Pillitteri guide](https://pasqualepillitteri.it/en/news/697/telegram-ai-editor-cocoon-guide)
- **"AI bot revolution" update (v12.7, May 2026).** Shipped Guest AI Bots, bot-to-bot communication, profile-level **Chat Automation**, custom AI styles, streaming bot replies and AI sticker search. **Guest Mode**: any bot that supports it can be @mentioned in private or group chats it is not a member of, and "only has access to the specific tagged message and any replies connected to it". **Chat Automation**: a bot can respond on a user's behalf, and the user configures which chats it can access, e.g. excluding contacts or limiting it to new chats — [Telegram blog: Guest AI Bots, Bot-to-Bot Chats, Chat Automation, Custom AI Styles…](https://telegram.org/blog/ai-bot-revolution-11-new-features); [MobiGyaan](https://www.mobigyaan.com/telegram-update-2026-ai-guest-bots); [TestingCatalog](https://www.testingcatalog.com/telegram-ships-major-update-for-ai-bots-and-automations/); [Gizbot](https://www.gizbot.com/apps/news/telegram-ai-tools-update-guest-bots-and-bot-communication-014-125495.html)

### Inferences
- Telegram's 2026 roadmap commoditizes the "basic" layer Mira sells: summaries, rewriting/translation, private inference, streaming and threads. Guest Mode lets *any* AI bot be summoned into *any* chat without being added. This both **lowers Mira's distribution friction** (users can @mira anywhere) and **erodes its group-presence moat** (any competitor can be @mentioned too).
- Guest Mode's built-in privacy scope (the bot sees only the tagged message and its replies) conflicts with Mira's "always-present group member with memory" model. Mira's group memory depends on being a full member with admin rights. That trade-off is a real design tension.
- Chat Automation turns "AI secretary that replies for you" into a platform primitive. The differentiator moves to *judgment*: what to auto-send, what to draft, what to escalate.
- Note that Mira, Cocoon, TON Tech and TOP's insights blog all sit in the same TOP/TON/Telegram orbit (TOP publishes the TON Tech Agentic Wallets post). Mira is effectively the ecosystem's flagship consumer agent, not an outsider.

- No reliable per-bot user counts for GPT4Telegrambot, Jadve or @askplexbot were found.

- I could not read Telegram's own May 2026 blog post in full (blocked). The Guest Mode and Chat Automation details come from secondary coverage that quotes it.
- "Custom AI Styles" is thinly described in the coverage.

## 3. Adjacent "AI agent in your messaging app" products

### Takeaway
Outside Telegram, the reference products are **Poke** (iMessage/SMS/Telegram/WhatsApp, proactive, playful onboarding), **Lindy Assistant** (iMessage executive assistant, expensive), **Meta AI** (default AI in WhatsApp, massive reach), **Toki** (calendar agent that moved off WhatsApp to Telegram/LINE), and **OpenClaw** (DIY power users). Meta's **January 15, 2026 ban on general-purpose chatbots in WhatsApp** pushed ChatGPT, Copilot and Perplexity out of WhatsApp. That makes **Telegram the most open large messenger for third-party AI agents**, a strategic tailwind for Mira.

### Cited Findings

- Later raised **$10M from Spark Capital at a $300M post-money valuation** (April 2026). TechCrunch (2026-04-08) described a 10-person team, and users composing automations in plain language and distributing them to other users — [AI News repost of TechCrunch](https://ainews.us.com/2026/04/08/poke-makes-ai-agents-as-easy-as-sending-a-text/); [StartupIntros](https://startupintros.com/news/2026-04-09-poke-venture-round)

## 5. Emerging UX best practices for conversational agents in messengers

### Takeaway
The 2025–2026 conventions are converging: **streamed and stoppable responses**, **threads/topics per task**, **mention-gated group participation**, **OAuth-in-chat connection links**, **approval cards before risky actions**, **proactive daily digests**, **shareable skills/automations**, **explicit memory scoping**, and **personality as product**. Telegram has made several of these platform primitives.

### Cited Findings
- **Streaming + stop.** Telegram natively supports streamed AI replies, and users can stop a draft at any point. Bots receive a `stopped_message_generation` update — [Telegram Bot Features](https://core.telegram.org/bots/features); [Telegram Bot API](https://core.telegram.org/bots/api). In private chats, draft streaming gives a typewriter effect without notification spam or edit rate limits. In groups, implementations fall back to send + edit — [AstrBot docs](https://docs.astrbot.app/en/platform/telegram.html). OpenClaw defaults to a single editable "progress" status draft showing tool progress, then sends the final answer — [OpenClaw Telegram messaging docs](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/blob/main/docs/channels/telegram/messaging.md)
- **Threads/topics as task containers.** Bot API 9.3 added topics in 1-on-1 bot chats so users can "organize bot chats into easily accessible threads" — [aiogram changelog](https://docs.aiogram.dev/en/v3.24.0/changelog.html). Mira keeps separate context per group topic — [Mira docs](https://github.com/DariaYakovleva/mira-wiki/blob/main/group-chats/overview.mdx). OpenClaw maps forum topics to sessions and agents — [OpenClaw docs](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/blob/main/docs/channels/telegram.md)
- **Summoning and scope in groups.** Telegram Guest Mode limits a summoned bot to the tagged message and its replies — [MobiGyaan](https://www.mobigyaan.com/telegram-update-2026-ai-guest-bots). Mira asks users to mention or reply in busy groups — [Mira docs](https://github.com/DariaYakovleva/mira-wiki/blob/main/group-chats/overview.mdx). OpenClaw uses mention gating and group allowlists — [OpenClaw docs](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/blob/main/docs/channels/telegram.md). Perplexity is tagged in groups to fact-check — [Gadgets To Use](https://gadgetstouse.com/blog/2025/04/12/use-perplexity-in-telegram)
- **Approvals before actions.** OpenClaw sends exec-approval cards with inline buttons to the approver's DM, optionally in the chat, which expire after 30 minutes. Reaction shortcuts are ✅ once / ♾️ always / ❌ deny — [OpenClaw rich messages](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/blob/main/docs/channels/telegram/rich-messages.md); [OpenClaw exec approvals](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/blob/main/docs/tools/exec-approvals.md). Telegram Business guidance recommends drafting replies for approval rather than auto-sending — [Telegram Bible](https://telegram-bible.pages.dev/guides/connect-claude-to-telegram-business). TON Agentic Wallets swap per-transaction approval for a **budgeted sub-wallet** — [Bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/ton-tech-gives-telegram-bots-spending-power-with-new-agentic-wallet-standard/); [Index.vn](https://index.vn/en/news/ton-launches-agentic-wallets-to-let-ai-agents-transact-on-chain-through-telegram)
- **Permission scoping for "reply on my behalf".** Telegram Chat Automation lets users choose which chats a bot can access, e.g. exclude contacts or only new chats — [TestingCatalog](https://www.testingcatalog.com/telegram-ships-major-update-for-ai-bots-and-automations/). Telegram Business bots need an explicit reply permission — [Chatplace](https://help.chatplace.io/en/articles/13593887-how-to-connect-the-ai-agent-to-a-telegram-business-account)
- **Pairing / allow-listing strangers.** OpenClaw pairs unknown DM senders by default and caps pending requests at 3 per channel — [OpenClaw pairing docs](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/blob/main/docs/channels/pairing.md)
- **Connect-in-chat.** Mira sends OAuth links in the chat — [Mira docs](https://github.com/DariaYakovleva/mira-wiki/blob/main/tools/overview.mdx). Poke users connect accounts and then just text — [Tech Funding News](https://techfundingnews.com/poke-launches-15m-seed-imessage-ai-assistant/)
- **Proactive digests and nudges.** Mira Daily — [Mira docs](https://github.com/DariaYakovleva/mira-wiki/blob/main/features/mira-daily.mdx). Poke's morning briefing and email nudges — [Notis](https://www.notis.ai/blog/poke-ai-review/). Lindy "acts on its own" — [Lindy](https://wf.lindy.ai/blog/lindy-assistant-launch)
- **Shareable automations.** Mira Skills share as t.me links with preview and one-tap install — [Mira docs](https://github.com/DariaYakovleva/mira-wiki/blob/main/skills/overview.mdx). Poke users distribute plain-language automations to others — [AI News/TechCrunch](https://ainews.us.com/2026/04/08/poke-makes-ai-agents-as-easy-as-sending-a-text/)
- **Style/personality controls.** Telegram AI Editor style presets — [NewsBytes](https://www.newsbytesapp.com/news/science/telegram-s-new-update-adds-ai-text-editor-improves-polls/story). Mira Daily's 6 styles and emoji preferences — [Mira docs](https://github.com/DariaYakovleva/mira-wiki/blob/main/features/mira-daily.mdx). Poke's personality-led onboarding — [ProductPep](https://www.productpep.com/blog/2025/11/16/its-finally-cool-to-poke). OpenClaw's casual, lowercase "friend" voice (see screenshot `competitor_openclaw_whatsapp-chat.jpg`)
- **Privacy modes as a visible toggle.** Mira Private Mode (Cocoon/TEE, Mini App toggle) — [Mira profile README](https://github.com/Mira-Agent/.github/blob/main/profile/README.md). Telegram positions all native AI on Cocoon — [Telegram blog](https://telegram.org/blog/new-design-ai-summaries)
- **Rich messages and Mini Apps.** Bot API 10.x rich messages and inline keyboards. OpenClaw notes that Mini App buttons only work in private chats — [OpenClaw rich messages](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/blob/main/docs/channels/telegram/rich-messages.md)
- **Ack reactions** (an emoji reaction to show "received/working") are a documented OpenClaw pattern — [OpenClaw Telegram messaging docs](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/blob/main/docs/channels/telegram/messaging.md)

### Inferences
- The emerging trust stack has three layers: (1) **who** can talk to the agent (pairing, allowlists), (2) **what** it can see (Guest-Mode scope, memory boundaries, chat-level permissions), and (3) **what it can do without asking** (approval cards, budgets, allow-once/always). Mira documents layer 2 well (its memory matrix) but publicly says little about layers 1 and 3.
- Status-draft streaming (one evolving message showing "Searching Gmail… drafting reply…") is becoming the expected "agent is working" affordance in Telegram. It doubles as transparency about tool use.

### Gaps
- No published usability research or metrics on which patterns (e.g. approval cards vs. budgets) actually improve trust or retention in messenger agents was found.

## 6. Whitespace: what no one does well yet

### Takeaway
The open opportunities sit where **multi-party chat, delegated action and trust** meet. Groups are still treated as "summarize/answer" surfaces, not places where an agent safely *acts for several people*. Approval and undo UX for consumer agents is immature outside developer tools. Memory is a black box to users. Proactivity is generic, not tied to the user's own data. Pricing for agents in messengers is confusing.

### Cited Findings (evidence of the gaps)
- Proactivity often disappoints: "Wasn't really useful and never proactive" — [Product Hunt Poke review](https://www.producthunt.com/p/poke-by-interaction-co/a-week-with-poke-review-a-promising-start-for-a-proactive-ai-assistant). Mira Daily's default content is weather, horoscope and news rather than the user's own inbox, calendar or groups — [Mira docs](https://github.com/DariaYakovleva/mira-wiki/blob/main/features/mira-daily.mdx)
- Agent money movement lacks clear liability and UX: Agentic Wallets let agents spend "without approval on every transaction", and commentators flag unaddressed liability — [Bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/ton-tech-gives-telegram-bots-spending-power-with-new-agentic-wallet-standard/); [MEXC](https://www.mexc.com/news/1096979). Mira's wallet docs show "Send 5 USDT to @username" with no documented confirmation step — [Mira profile README](https://github.com/Mira-Agent/.github/blob/main/profile/README.md)
- Strong approval UX exists today mostly in developer tools (OpenClaw exec approvals) — [OpenClaw docs](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/blob/main/docs/tools/exec-approvals.md). Self-hosted setups carry real security risk (exposed instances leaking keys) — [eWeek](https://www.eweek.com/news/clawdbot-self-hosted-ai-security-risks-neuron/)
- Pricing confusion around tokens vs. subscriptions — [T—Zh comment](https://t-j.ru/news/telegram-mira/); conflicting Mira tiers — [mira-pro.com](https://mira-pro.com/) vs [mira.tg](https://mira.tg/compare/mira-vs-grok). Poke experimented with negotiated pricing — [Layer3 Labs](https://www.layer3labs.io/guides/poke-ai-explained)
- Privacy skepticism ("privacy or just marketing?") despite Cocoon — [VGTimes](https://vgtimes.ru/tech-and-hardware/147319-v-telegram-zapuschen-ii-assistent-mira-privatnost-ili-prosto-marketing.html)
- Platform risk for agents on closed messengers: the WhatsApp ban and Toki's forced exit — [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/meta-removes-rival-chatbots-from-whatsapp/); [Toki](https://toki.com/fr/whatsapp-notice)
- Telegram's Guest Mode restricts summoned bots to the tagged message and replies. This creates an unresolved design question about how a "group-memory" agent gets and earns broader context — [MobiGyaan](https://www.mobigyaan.com/telegram-update-2026-ai-guest-bots)

### Inferences (opportunities a Mira proposal could claim)
1. **Group consent and roles.** Nobody designs for *multi-party consent*: who in a group can ask the agent to act (send invites, spend from a shared wallet, post to a channel), and how other members see, approve or veto. Possible patterns: group-visible task cards with an "approve by 2 admins" mechanic, and per-member memory opt-outs.
2. **Consumer-grade approval cards.** Bring OpenClaw-style allow once / always / deny, expiry and DM-routed approvals to normal users. Use Telegram inline buttons and rich messages with a plain-language "what will happen" preview, plus **undo windows** for sends and payments. Tie this to TON budgets ("Mira can spend up to 20 TON/week without asking").
3. **Transparent, editable memory.** Mira's privacy matrix is a strong policy, but users cannot *see* it at work. A "What Mira remembers about this group" card or Mini App view, with per-fact delete and "forget this" in-line, would make the moat tangible and answer the "just marketing?" critique.
4. **Proactivity grounded in the user's own signals.** Move the daily digest from horoscope/news to "3 things in your chats you missed + 2 decisions waiting on you + 1 calendar conflict". Use Telegram's own surfaces (unread groups, mentions) that only an in-Telegram agent can see.
5. **Secretary mode built on Chat Automation.** Telegram made reply-on-my-behalf a primitive. The open question is *judgment UX*: a triage inbox (auto-handled / drafted for you / needs you), tone presets per contact, and a weekly "here's what I said for you" audit.
6. **Pricing legibility.** A single clear meter (Stars-based, shown in-chat before expensive actions like video generation) instead of mixed tokens, Stars and USD tiers.
7. **Cross-messenger portability as a hedge.** Given WhatsApp's ban and iMessage gating, a "Telegram-first, portable memory" story (an iMessage version is on Mira's roadmap) is a differentiator against Telegram's native AI, which will never leave Telegram.

### Gaps
- These opportunities are inferred from documentation and reviews, not validated with user research. No quantitative data on group-agent adoption or failure modes was found.
